ABOUT VOICE OF THE PEOPLE

The Voice Of The People is a web-based project that aims to provide a platform for citizens to report problems and issues related to their city or municipality.This project is designed to make it easy for citizens to submit complaints by attaching images and other relevant information and receive updates on the resolution of their complaints such as potholes,streetlights problems,garbage problems etc.This project allows the municipal corporation to track the status of complaints,assign complaints to appropriate departments or officials,and update citizens on the resolution of their complaints.By providing citizens with an easy and efficient way to report problems,and by allowing the municipal corporation to manage complaints efficiently, this project aims to improve the overall quality of life in the city or municipality.
